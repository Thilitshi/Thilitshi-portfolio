GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I am a recent BSc Computer Science graduate from the University of the Western Cape, with a strong foundation in programming, problem-solving, algorithms, data structures, software engineering, databases, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer systems. My studies gave me both theoretical knowledge and practical experience in using technology to design, develop, and solve real-world problems.

Throughout my studies and personal development, I have worked on projects across different areas of technology. My favourite project is ProjectSync, a full-stack collaboration platform designed to help developers share project progress and connect with potential teammates. I also built a Raspberry Pi traffic light simulation using Python and a Hotel Booking System that strengthened my understanding of database design, SQL, and data integrity.

My main interests are software development, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent applications, automating processes, analysing data, and creating practical solutions to real-world problems. I continue to develop my knowledge through certifications, personal projects, and self-directed learning in areas such as AI, Data Science, and Cloud Computing.

Beyond technology, I value teamwork, communication, continuous learning, and community. I am fluent in English and Tshivenda, and my volunteer experience at Dzwerani Lutheran Church strengthened my communication, research, organisation, and ability to support others. I am a curious, adaptable, and motivated individual who enjoys solving problems and taking on new challenges. My goal is to grow as a technology professional, gain valuable industry experience, and contribute to innovative projects where I can combine technical skills, creativity, AI, data, and problem-solving to create meaningful solutions.

I am always learning. I am always building. And I am always looking for the next problem worth solving.
